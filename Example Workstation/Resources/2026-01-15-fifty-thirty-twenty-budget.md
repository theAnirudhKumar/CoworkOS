# The 50/30/20 Budget Rule

*Example saved note inside the example workstation, showing the shape rule 26 in 00_Reference/File Creation Rules.md asks for. Replace or delete it with the rest of this folder.*

**Why it was saved:** a simple starting split for the monthly budget, while the spending categories are still being worked out.

**Key points**

- **Split take-home pay three ways: about half for needs, under a third for wants, and at least a fifth for savings and paying down debt.**
- "Needs" means what you would still pay after losing your job: rent, groceries, insurance, minimum loan payments.
- The split is a starting point. The article says most people adjust it after two or three months of real numbers.
- Savings come out first, on payday, so they do not depend on what is left at the end of the month.

**Source:** a personal-finance article saved on 2026-01-15. The figures are the article's own and were not checked.

**Use it for:** setting up or resetting the monthly budget, and the yearly money check-in.
