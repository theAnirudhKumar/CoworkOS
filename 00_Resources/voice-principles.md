# Voice Principles

Read this before producing any written content on the user's behalf: emails, documents, social posts, anything meant to sound like them rather than like Claude. It ships empty on purpose. Fill it in as you learn how the user actually wants their writing to sound, the same way MEMORY.md fills in through use rather than being pre-populated.

---

## How to fill this in

Do not write a full voice guide up front by guessing. Build it the way the rest of this workspace builds itself: when a piece of writing gets corrected, that correction usually reveals a rule. "Too formal, make it sound like I'm talking to a colleague" is a tone rule. "Cut the exclamation points" is a punctuation rule. "This should be three sentences, not three paragraphs" is a length rule. Each of those is a capture trigger under the Memory System, the same as any other correction, and belongs here rather than in MEMORY.md because it prescribes behavior rather than describing a fact.

## Tone

*How formal or casual, how warm or direct, first person or not. Empty until stated.*

## Structure and length

*Default length for an email, a document, a social post. Preference for short paragraphs versus long ones, bullet points versus prose, how a piece should open and close.*

## Words and phrases to avoid

*Anything the user has flagged as not sounding like them: corporate jargon, particular filler words, a greeting or sign-off they dislike.*

## Formatting defaults

*Bold and italics usage, how lists get used (or avoided), punctuation preferences such as em dash versus hyphen.*

## Examples

*Once you have two or three pieces of writing the user has approved without heavy edits, note what made them work here. A short "before/after" from an actual correction is worth more than an abstract rule.*
